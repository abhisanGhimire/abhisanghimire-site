Preprint

Social Determinants of Health & Fentanyl Overdose Mortality

Question: Which county-level social determinants predict fentanyl overdose mortality, and where should prevention resources go first?

Approach: XGBoost model with SHAP interpretation over 975 U.S. counties, combining CDC WONDER, Social Vulnerability Index, health behavior, and healthcare workforce data

Technologies: Python, XGBoost, SHAP

My contribution: Co-author, modeling & analysis

Status: Preprint · arXiv:2605.08230

Preprint

Wearable-Grade Lead Reduction & ECG AI Performance in Elderly Patients

Question: Does reducing clinical 12-lead ECGs to a single wearable-grade lead degrade AI diagnostic accuracy differently across age groups?

Approach: 1D residual neural network trained and validated on 21,091 PTB-XL and MIT-BIH recordings, benchmarked across lead configurations and age groups

Technologies: Python, deep learning, PTB-XL, MIT-BIH

My contribution: Co-author, modeling & analysis

Status: Preprint · medRxiv

Publication

Understanding Hate Group Videos on YouTube

Question: How do U.S. hate groups present themselves and frame social problems in YouTube videos?

Approach: Grounded-theory analysis of 96 videos via open-coding, affinity diagramming, and collective-action framing analysis

Technologies: Python, research data tooling

My contribution: Data collection and Python-based research tooling

Status: Published · ACM CSCW'22 Companion
